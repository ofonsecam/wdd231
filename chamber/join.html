Non-Profit Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: Free ($0 COP)

  • Standard listing in the Funza Chamber online directory.
  • Access to monthly community networking breakfast.
  • Eligibility to post community events on the local board.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: $350,000 COP

  • All Non-Profit tier benefits included.
  • 10% discount on Chamber workshops and logistics seminars.
  • Social media welcome mention across Chamber channels.

Silver Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: $750,000 COP

  • All Bronze tier benefits included.
  • 25% discount on trade shows, booth rentals, and executive training.
  • Quarterly feature in the Sabana Occidente business newsletter.

Gold Membership Benefits

Annual Fee: $1,400,000 COP

  • All Silver tier benefits included.
  • Rotating spotlights placement on the Chamber Home Page.
  • VIP invitations to regional government and industry roundtables.
  • 50% discount on all Chamber events and sponsorship packages.
