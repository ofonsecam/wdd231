Discover Funza, Cundinamarca

Area Overview & Demographics

Located in the Sabana Occidente province just west of Bogotá, Funza is a dynamic commercial, agricultural, and logistics center.

  • Population: ~112,000 residents
  • Elevation: 2,548 meters above sea level
  • Key Industries: Logistics, Floriculture, Food Processing & Retail
  • Strategic Access: Direct connection to Calle 13 & Autopista Medellín
